Foreword: Why Armenia’s Case Is Different?

You have read libertarian books before. They tell you the state is too big. They tell you to cut taxes by 20%, audit the Fed, abolish three agencies, and wait a generation. This is not that book.

This manifesto was written in a country that might not exist in 10-20 years.

The Stakes.

I am writing from Yerevan, Armenia. Forty kilometers from here, Azerbaijan’s army stands on land it took from us in 2020 and 2023. Turkey, its ally, declares openly that its final goal is a pan-Turkic corridor that erases Armenia from the map. Russia, our “guarantor,” watched. The West sent tweets.

We have a bit less than 30,000 square kilometers left. We have a bit less than 3 million people. We have a state that consumes almost the whole of GDP and still cannot defend a village. In the West, “big government” means slow internet at the DMV. Here, “big government” means you will not have a country.

THE DIAGNOSIS.

Every failed state blames foreigners. I don’t. Our collapse is domestic. It is the cult of the state.

For 70 years we were Soviet. For 36 years after, we replaced the hammer-and-sickle with a three-colour flag, but kept the same “religion”: the state is sacred, the state will save us, the state must be obeyed.

The state is not sacred. The state is the ruling gang. We hired them as servants. They became our masters. They tax us without our consent, print money and call it “inflation,” disarm us and call it “security,” and tell us to die for borders they sell at night.

This is not theory. This is Article 60 of our Constitution: the state has a monopoly on taxation; There are many other articles of our constitution: the state is social and so on, which mean nothing certain. This is our Central Bank printing money while our soldiers buy drones with donations. This is our Parliament voting itself immunity while our villages are shelled. This is us who is always trying to make the elections just and elect a Christ-like saviour. And we don’t understand that there cant be any. Neither the first, nor the second.

THE SOLUTION.

You cannot reform this. You cannot “vote harder.” You dissolve it.

This manifesto proposes 7 steps. Abolish the National Assembly. Abolish 90% of ministries. Abolish the Central Bank. Abolish the tax service. Cap taxes at 0–10%. Return arms to the citizen. Make courts elected and removable by the people.

It sounds insane. It is not. It is bookkeeping. A state that cannot defend its borders has no right to almost 30% (and even more) of your labor. A state that loses wars must be reduced to two functions: protection and justice. Everything else must be returned to private hands, under the four axioms of morality: property, contract, truth, equality before law.

WHY YOU SHOULD READ THIS.

If you are American, you think “tyranny” is the IRS. I will show you tyranny: when the IRS and the enemy army are the same threat. If you are Argentine, you know inflation. I will show you inflation when the enemy is at the gate and the Central Bank is printing for both. If you are Lebanese, Haitian, or any citizen of a failed state, this is your template. This is not “privatize the railroads.” This is “dissolve the parliament before the country dissolves.”

The West wrote libertarianism for rich, safe countries. I wrote it for a country bleeding out. That is why it is 19 pages, not 600. You don’t read Mises in an ambulance.

If we fail, there will be no Armenia to write about. If we succeed, there will be a model for every nation that is told “you have no choice but to die slowly.”

You have a choice.

MANIFESTO OF SALVATION

The Diagnosis – Armenia Is on the Brink of Collapse – SOS!

A mortal danger threatens the Republic of Armenia today. The cause is not external enemies, but the domestic constitutional order, which grants the state’s ruling apparatus nearly unlimited rights, and the “short-sightedness” of our intelligentsia, which does not consider or does not know this exposition, so as to explain it to ordinary individuals. The ruling apparatus of the state has proclaimed itself “the state” and even “the fatherland,” and, by the one-man will of its prime minister who is supported by the ruling political party, acts contrary to the four basic norms of morality and justice.

The Four Norms of Morality and Justice

Articles one and two of the “FOUNDATIONS OF THE CONSTITUTIONAL ORDER” chapter of the RA Constitution state:

“The Republic of Armenia is a sovereign, democratic, social, rule-of-law state.”

The meaning of this declaration is completely unknown: for first, it does not become clear what “social society” is, and second, it is unknown what “the state’s rule-of-law, democracy, and sovereignty” are and how they are expressed. For example, a termite mound or an anthill is also a “social society.” So what?

And if by the word “social” the so-called “social justice” is meant, then – as shown below – that is social injustice itself. Or, as F. Hayek showed in the “The Mirage of Social Justice” volume of his “Law, Legislation and Liberty” trilogy, social justice is a mirage or illusion.

In the RA Constitution, the number of uncertainties, deficiencies, and also contradictions like this exceeds one hundred, and it is completely impossible that with such a constitution a moral and just social order could be defined and established.

Any moral Constitution must first proclaim that the state it establishes is an independent and sovereign state, and that it establishes its constitutional order and laws independently, by referenda of its own people, and does not submit to the instructions of any foreign state or foreign structure. The RA Constitution does not!

Besides, it is impossible that the collective concept “people” be ascribed the qualities of an individual. Isn’t it obvious that: “The people sneezed, then, by its own will, wiped its nose and laughed” is meaningless speech? For this reason, the true constitution of the RA must define a social order such that is, indeed, moral and just, and where every single thing is done by individuals, and not by this “abstract and undefined state” thing.

Any moral and just social order is based on the following 4 axioms:

I. The Inviolability of Private Property. No one, not even the state, has the right to dispose (seize) of a citizen’s property without the voluntary consent of that citizen. The forcible seizure of private property is plunder.

II. The Inviolability of Contract. Properties are exchanged only by the bilateral voluntary condition of the individual owners, and not by force or threat.

III. The Prohibition of Lying and Breaking Promises. The lie of a state official or the breaking of a promise is state treason and must be punished by criminal laws.

IV. Protected Free Competition. All are equal before the law.

No monopoly, no privilege, no bonus, no immunity for any official, no bodyguards and motorcades kept at the expense of the state budget must exist. Violating these norms and their consequences eliminates both morality and justice, and humanity itself.

Caution.

This manifesto is only an exposition of the general direction and general spirit of actions. This is neither a program nor a constitution. Nor is this a technical collection of laws.

It is obvious that without a transition period no reform is possible. And there must be defined, for example, a three-year (or five-year) transition period, during which:

1. All state pensions, benefits, disability and loss compensations must be preserved – without reduction.

2. Current state payments for healthcare and social insurance must be preserved.

3. These expenses will be financed from the property and budgets of the dissolved ministries, city halls, the Central Bank, the SRC (the State Revenue Committee), and other large state structures, as well as by funds obtained from the privatization of state real estate, and during the Transition period no new tax or duty must be established.

4. Citizenship of the Republic of Armenia shall also not be granted to migrants coming from states whose religious or traditional views are contrary to the religious or traditional views of the citizens of the Republic of Armenia, since, as the experience of Europe demonstrates, such migrants do not integrate into the religious and traditional reality of the Republic of Armenia. Migrants shall be prohibited from receiving any state social assistance.

5. Private property rights of disposition shall be restricted by prohibiting the sale or transfer in any form of privately owned land to citizens of other states, as long as there remains a theoretical danger of usurping the territory of the Republic of Armenia, such as plans for the creation of a Turanian imperial state by Turkey.

6. The transfer to another state of privately owned lands purchased from citizens with the consent of those citizens and at the price set by those citizens is possible only and solely by the decision of a nationwide referendum. All state officials who violate this rule shall be immediately dismissed from their positions without any delays or bureaucratic red tape and shall be brought to trial.

After the transition period, the system of accumulating pensions, gradually and by a procedure approved by referenda, must pass to private voluntary cumulative insurance.

However, those large state pensions that the ruling apparatus has established for state officials by the force of its “laws” (for example, the enormous pensions of generals, colonels, ministers, judges and prosecutors, etc.) must be abolished and become equal to the pensions of ordinary citizens.

The Security of the Transition Period

a. Citizenship. RA citizenship is not granted to citizens of those states with which the RA is in a state of war or unsettled conflict, or whose official doctrine contains claims directed against the territorial integrity of the RA, and whose ideological doctrines are opposed to the customary norms of the RA, and this opposition is irremovable.

The list of these states is approved by local referenda.

b. Social payments. Only citizens of the RA have the right to receive a pension, benefit, or other social assistance from the budget of the RA Government.

c. Real estate. The procedure for foreign citizens and companies to acquire land and real estate in the RA is established only by local referendum. Without the consent of the community, a transaction to acquire land and real estate is null and void.

Today’s Order of the RA Is Predatory Socialism

a. Tax and customs are plunder, if they are established without the consent of the private property owner. Today’s Constitution, by Article 60, gives the state a monopoly on plunder.

b. The state is elected to be our servant, but today it has become our master. We gave the state the right to disarm us, but itself remain armed, to secretly dispose of our very property, to listen to our phones, to seize our land and houses – under the predatory pretext of “the overriding interest of the state.”

c. It is the Central Bank (the CB) that counterfeits money and causes inflation. The CB is among the very first enemies of our people. The monopoly on currency manipulation, on taking external and especially internal debt and placing it on the shoulders of our generations, as well as on printing paper money and increasing the money supply (that is, counterfeiting money), and therefore also the monopoly on inflation, belongs to the CB. The inevitable consequence of inflation is the unceasing price rise that plunders the people, which at any moment threatens hyperinflation. The dollar and the other currencies, as well as gold, are economic goods, and when the CB supposedly regulates their prices, it contradicts science, it plunders, and it obstructs export-import. The CB, by this supposed regulation, further impoverishes the already poor and further enriches the already rich. Therefore, such regulations of CB must be prohibited.

d. For the same reasons, the powers of the Constitutional Court (the CC) and the Supreme Judicial Council (the SJC) must be given to ordinary courts. Isn’t it obvious that if a judge is incapable of interpreting any article of the constitution, then either that article of the constitution is of no use, or the judge is? The existence of the SJC is completely meaningless, for if the court and the Constitution exist, is there, besides their justice, also some “supreme justice”?

e. There must be no SRC, because when we hire the state-ruling apparatus – by electing it for some salary – we hire it to serve us, to collect tax and customs. This servant of ours, besides tax and customs, must have no other income, the income which, actually, belongs to us only, not the state.

The Formulae and Steps of Salvation

We need money to have a powerful army to protect us from any intervention. For this, the following steps must be taken.

Step I. The minimal state must be established, on the following foundations: The ruling apparatus is an evil. If this evil is unavoidable in the present world, it must be the minimum. To bring this evil to the minimum, the state must be decentralized as much as it is possible and reasonable in the state of the present global situation.

a. The following must be dissolved: The National Assembly (NA), about 90% of the about 40 ministries and other very large state institutions of ministerial weight, and the city halls.

Dissolving city halls eliminates their possibility of engaging in any economic (and inevitably corrupt) activity today. Community halls and village halls must also be deprived of this possibility, because almost all of their functions must be given to private hands. The remaining administrative functions of these must be transferred to local (with a strictly limited low number of employees) community halls, to local referenda, as well as to sheriffs and courts elected by those referenda. This will forever eliminate the monopoly corruption of distributing licenses (for bribe) of: “parking lots, garbage collection, advertising billboards, and other such building permits.”

(The NA is a legislative organ, and the member of a legislative organ is, in fact, a “judge examining and stating the fairness of a law,” and this member must be free from party bias. It follows that the existence of party factions in the NA is contrary to the constitutional norm of equality before the law. As the parties are inevitable and the courts have the right of producing law, the NA must be dissolved).

b. Perhaps only the ministries of foreign affairs, defense, and the courts elected and dissolved by local referenda must remain.

c. All state institutions and officials must be subject to an audit appointed by the court and give an annual report to that court, as it was in Ancient Athens.

d. The powers and rights of all state institutions must be drastically reduced and almost all of their functions must be given to private hands. Except for a referendum, the government or any other state organ must not have the right to declare war or to surrender. This function must be given only to a referendum.

e. Citizens have the right to sue any state official and legal entity (especially the prime minister and members of the government or a minister and the ministries, etc.) if they collect, for instance, 10000 signatures. Upon the first case of LIE or corruption proven by a court, they must be immediately dismissed from office, without any impeachment or other formality. A judicial claim must be filed against them, especially if during their office they disposed of citizens’ lives or large state resources. That court which does not accept a claim of this lie or of damaging another’s private property, or delays the proceedings, must be immediately dissolved, and its judges must also be put on trial. But if the citizen’s accusation in the claim is not proven, the plaintiff citizen himself (as in Ancient Athens) is put on trial.

Step II. The sanctity of private property must be established.

a. The “law of the overriding interest of the state” must be abolished. Not even one square centimeter of land may be taken from the owner without the owner’s voluntary consent. The issue of disposing of and alienating land considered public is decided only by local referendum.

b. By referenda, the property tax, movable property tax must be abolished. The tax on small business must become zero. The tax on medium business must not exceed 5%, for the rest a flat tax of no more than 10% must be established.

Step III. The citizen must be armed.

a. Every sane citizen has the right to possess arms and to carry a pistol.

b. The puppet institution of the president must be abolished.

c. The Prime Minister/President should not be the Supreme Commander-in-Chief (CIC), or any other type of military commander, by virtue of the constitution (or otherwise), unless he has been elected by referendum; nor should he have the right to appoint anyone to a military office unless he is the CIC. Candidates for military offices should be elected democratically. The voters should be all military personnel. The elections should be conducted by open ballot and should be direct and based on a majoritarian system.

d. Private military units are permitted, up to and including regiments (“Yerkrapah”-like, but independ¬ent of the state), as well as private police divisions. See the commentaries. Private military and police units are also subject to the law.

Step IV. The court becomes single-tier.

a. Justice is completely separated from the state and becomes the function of courts elected and dismissed by local referenda. The judge is elected and dismissed by the referendum of the local community, when a need arises (for example, by an initiative of 1,000 or 1,500 signatures). A judge dismissed from office must immediately and automatically be put on trial.

b. For grave crimes, the institution of jurors is established.

c. Military courts are dissolved – no specific justice, for all are equal before the law.

The courts must receive a certain sum by a certain mechanism from every trial – somewhat lake lawyers – ¬which will create an incentive for a given court to have many plaintiffs and will, of course, also force courts to compete to establish and strengthen their honest reputation.

Step V. Legislation must be separated from the state.

a. The NA functions of legislation must be handed to the courts. They must judge by the basic theses of Roman common law, and the jurors must decide by the logic of the norms of morality and justice.

b. Newly produced legislation must become fully precedential.

c. The state must be deprived of the right to propose or establish a draft law or normative act. The ~40,000 laws adopted in the last 25–30 years by “state drafts,” almost entirely, must be dissolved, so that the market becomes free.

Step VI. The police must be subordinate only to the local community.

a. The institution of sheriffs must be established, whom only the community referendum elects and dismisses.

b. The institution of sheriffs must be established on the model of the sheriffs of the USA. The sheriff must be elected and dismissed like a judge.

The sheriff and the community police must be subordinate only to the local community (for example, to the local referendum), and not subordinate to any other institution, not even the prime minister. Anyhow, a sheriff must be subject to a court trial.

c. The police huge army of about more than half the state army must be reduced to a reasonably low number.

Step VII. The state secret does only hide the state crimes committed by the ruling apparatus-the state, especially the most horrifying and filthy of them, from the people (for example, those secrets which show the grave crimes of high state officials, the Central Bank, the National Security Service (NSS), and the remaining most important institutions of the state).

a. The state secret must be abolished (except for military secrets during days of war).

b. Arms trade must not be a state secret, because in today’s technological world it is absolutely impossible for large trade to remain secret.

c. Any type of secret discussions and trials and hearings must be prohibited.

The psychology of the people must change. We must abandon the illusion of “the state is sacred” and “the leader will save us.” There cannot be a just state, there cannot be a Christ-like saviour who could have saved us without changing the social order of the society according to the above stated moral norms. There is only the free person, the free market, free people according to moral norms.

CONCLUSION: Either the state is powerful and rich, or the people are. There is no third option.

If the ideas of this Manifesto and its explanations become the imperative of the day, the RA will not vanish from the map and we will have money, an army, freedom, and there will be a future.

If they do not, there is no future and there will not be, and the main culprit will be us.

COMMENTARIES

Armenia’s situation is critical. It is not excluded that the Republic of Armenia (RA) may vanish from the map. For five years I have been trying to tell our educated class what the RA needs to survive, drawing on Austrian economics – praxeology. This manifesto is only a synopsis of the theories of Ludwig von Mises and Friedrich August von Hayek. For detail see my The State versus the People or Moral Order – the Saviour and the bibliography in there – more than 60 English titles – 2021, 480 pp; and The Country Rescue Formulae, 2024, 168 pp, Edit Print, Yerevan.

First, a constitution must contain several mandatory laws, and several laws it must not contain.

For example, “the law of the overriding interest of the state” lets the ruling apparatus seize any citizen’s private land – at a price the apparatus itself sets. That is immoral and unjust, because seizure is plunder. This law serves only the overriding interests of the rulers, not the citizen’s. It is by this law that homes in Yerevan’s Kond district are being taken today. It is by this law that private lands of RA citizens are handed to Azerbaijanis.

State secret. Isn’t it absurd that our hired “servants” keep secrets about how they dispose of our property? We hire a servant by election, not a ruler who then calls himself “authority” and “minister.”

All immunities of our servant-rulers must be abolished: all bonuses, all privileges, all bodyguards funded by the state, all motorcades, all official cars, all state apartments and free meals. No official’s salary may exceed a professor’s salary.

Rulers must go from home to work and back on public transport, at their own expense. Let the official know his post is dangerous – that ordinary citizens may shoot him for immoral and unjust conduct.

MORALITY AND JUSTICE

A free market (or free-market relations) rests on the basic norms of morality and creates a moral order. Again, the foundation of a moral order is the free market, or a truly capitalist order. A moral order is also a just order. To this day, true capitalism has never been fully implemented in any country; capitalism has always been mixed with socialism.

Here are the four norms of morality and justice. Violating them must be treated as state treason.

When the Constitution or anyone else asserts that “the Republic of Armenia is a state of independent, free, and sovereign citizens,” the assertion is, in fact, devoid of any real or substantive meaning. Why? Because the concept of the “Republic of Armenia” is a collective concept. It is a mistake to attribute the properties of an individual to a collective entity. Only an individual can be independent and sovereign, not a collective abstraction.

The independence and freedom of a member of society consist, first and foremost, in the inviolability of that individual's private property and the freedom to dispose of that property as they see fit.

Yet the current Constitution of Armenia states that the Republic of Armenia is an independent, free, and sovereign state, which contradicts reality. Is it not the case that, following the defeat of 2020, the Republic of Armenia has been subject to the will of Aliyev and other well-known and unknown individuals?

A realistic, just, and moral constitution must first declare that the Republic of Armenia is a state of independent, free, and sovereign citizens, that its social order and the fundamental laws governing that order are established through referendums held among its own citizens, and that it is not subject to the directives of any (foreign, as well) institution or individual.

In the sense explained above, any just social order rests on the following four axioms:

I. The inviolability of private property. No one, including the state, has the right to dispose of a citizen's property—let alone seize it—without that citizen's voluntary consent. Forcibly taking control of a citizen's property or seizing it outright is plunder.

II. The inviolability of contracts. Citizens' property may be exchanged only by mutual voluntary agreement between its owners, never through force or threats.

III. The prohibition of lying and breaking promises. A public official's lie or breach of promise constitutes treason against the state and must be punished under criminal law. The minimum penalty is the immediate dismissal of the lying public official.

IV. Protected free competition. Everyone is equal before the law. There must be no monopoly whatsoever over property or rights, no privileges, no bonuses, no preferential salaries or immunity for public officials, and no bodyguards or motorcades. Violations of these and similar principles destroy morality, justice, and humanity alike.

In fact, these four norms are fused into one another. Only a social order that satisfies these norms – and only these norms – is moral and just, because all morality and justice we know, all that is human and just, derives, more or less, from these four basic norms.

When these norms are not followed, both morality and justice vanish; what is bad and vile is called good and excellent; and humanity itself is annihilated. When these norms are not followed, human society turns into something far worse than a pack ruled by laws more unjust than a bandit’s, and it disintegrates. Civilization itself disappears. John Locke said four centuries ago:

Theorem 1. Abolish private property and you will abolish justice.

The source of human justice is the inviolability of private property alone. The only alternative to justice is injustice, and the only source of injustice is violence and deceit – sword and cannon, or the fraudulent and treacherous propaganda of lies.

AXIOM 1. From the VERY BEGINNING of a moral constitution these four moral norms must have been laid down, because only the constitutional, criminal, and civil-administrative laws that implement these – and derive from them – will allow a truly moral and just capitalism to take root in a country governed by them.

AXIOM 2. The aim of a moral constitution must be to implement these four moral norms in the country as completely as possible. Only by these norms must a moral and just order – i.e. public justice, or true capitalism – be established.

The truly moral and just capitalism has been mankind’s long-standing dream. The more fully the basic norms of morality are implemented, the more political and economic freedom and welfare every individual in that society will have; and the production and quality of spiritual and material goods will be the greatest possible, while prices and misery will be the least possible.

Truly moral and just capitalism is an extremely fragile order. Because for about a century and a half, socialists have slandered and discredited capitalism, painting it as unjust and bloodthirsty. Whereas, IT IS SOCIALISM THAT IS UNJUST AND BLOODTHIRSTY.

From these four norms flow all the moral-legal and ethical rules we follow at every step, almost by instinct – for example, the reflex of apologizing when we step on someone’s foot.
